A first municipal pilot

Institutional gardens with explicit harvest permissions.

  1. Identify an actual parcel and confirm who owns every tree.
  2. Conduct a seasonal tree inventory; record genus, species, canopy role and structural health.
  3. Review written permissions, phytosanitary controls, climate fit and soil history.
  4. Choose a small professional trial only if a specific compatible host and tested scion are found.
  5. Publish survival, harvest, fruit safety, access and maintenance outcomes over multiple seasons.
