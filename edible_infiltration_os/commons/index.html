Decentralized commons

Opt-in peer-to-peer coordination, with explicit consent and offline JSON fallback.

VOLUNTARY PEER-TO-PEER / NOSTR SIGNALING

Collaborate without a central archive

On a hosted HTTPS site, peers using the same room code can exchange short planning notes through opt-in WebRTC (Trystero). Rooms use public Nostr relays for discovery. Peer identities/IP information may be disclosed to counterparties; do not share sensitive locations, personal data or unapproved fieldwork. Nothing is broadcast until you press Connect or Send.

Offline by design · not connected.

Messages are ephemeral unless you choose to save/export them. No central history or automatic cross-device sync.

Decentralized does not mean automatically safe

Strengths

No account requirement or permanent central database; portable local work; room-based exchange when peers are available.

Limits

Active peers need internet and compatible networking; public signaling services can fail. Room membership is not identity verification, and messages are not moderation-reviewed.

Zero-harm · Anti-inversion. No unauthorized cuts, forced sharing, ecological introductions or unsupported claims. This is a planning archive, not a live tree survey or planting permit.
