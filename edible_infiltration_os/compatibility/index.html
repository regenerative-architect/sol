Do not confuse a graft with a genetic modification

A graft is a physical union of two plants; the scion keeps its fruiting identity. The rootstock influences vigor, water uptake and other traits but does not automatically make the scion frost-tolerant or disease-free.

Zero-harm · Anti-inversion. No unauthorized cuts, forced sharing, ecological introductions or unsupported claims. This is a planning archive, not a live tree survey or planting permit.
