BIOLOGICAL HARD STOP

Maple trees do not turn into apple trees.

Grafting transfers a living branch onto compatible vascular tissue. An apple cultivar grafted to suitable Malus material can make a fruiting branch. An apple grafted to maple, oak, elm or pine cannot form a practical lasting graft union.

Learn the science
Common misconception

Compatible is not the same as safe, legal or productive. A successful first-season bud does not prove long-term compatibility or a suitable public harvest.
