Evidence tiers and known limits

Established

Documented common graft route for an identified rootstock and scion group. Still needs cultivar and site checks.

Conditional

Supported in some settings but dependent on exact plant material, season, pest context and possibly interstocks.

Unverified / incompatible

Unknown is not impossible, and shared family is not sufficient. Obvious cross-family nonmatches are screened as incompatible.

What this archive does not claim

It does not measure city food insecurity, identify available municipal host trees, audit laws jurisdiction by jurisdiction, or guarantee crop production. The city entries are planning hypotheses that require independent local validation before planting.

Zero-harm · Anti-inversion. No unauthorized cuts, forced sharing, ecological introductions or unsupported claims. This is a planning archive, not a live tree survey or planting permit.
