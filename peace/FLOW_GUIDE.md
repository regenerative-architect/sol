# SOL Peace Commons v2.2 — one flow, live peer presence, local intelligence HUD

## Start

Open the master app. The introduction has only one main action, **Begin with a finding**. The five-phase experience remains **Begin → Explore → Reflect → Build → Continue**. There is no class selection, questionnaire or skill-tree administration required to start.

**Earth Intelligence Engine** is in the compact HUD at the top. Open it for a source-grounded local assessment; close it to return to the same place in the flow. The default **Trystero** status appears beside it. On a hosted, online page the app attempts to connect to a public research room automatically; you can disconnect immediately and opt out of future auto-joins. No research content is auto-shared.

## Tips

- **🔎 Verify before awarding confidence:** a matching source is a research lead, not confirmation that the claim is current or independently corroborated.
- **🕊️ Work from civilian outcomes:** measure access, harm reduction, independent monitoring and legal authority.
- **🧭 Follow sources, not badges:** educational classes and XP do not confer agency membership, approval or special permissions.
- **🧩 Combine viewpoints:** the CIA-, FBI-, Secret Service-, military- and UN-themed editions are public-interest learning lenses, not real institutional participants.
- **↧ Keep backups:** export a private JSON backup, and only create public handoffs for records you explicitly choose to release.

Read `EIE_HUD_GUIDE.md` for the local analytic pipeline and `PEER_RUNBOOK.md` for connectivity/privacy controls. All original archived modules are accessible through the research menu. Uncached outside sites and peer communication require an internet connection; local EIE retrieval does not.
