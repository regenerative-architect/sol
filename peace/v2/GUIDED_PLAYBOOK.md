# 10-minute Convergence Worlds playbook

**0–2 min — Explore a perspective.** On the Living Dashboard, press UN, FBI, CIA, USSS or USMC. A small report changes **inside the existing panel**. No account or website switching is needed. The buttons represent public-interest topics, not access to the agencies themselves. **Pro tip:** notice the date and the limit of each public-source claim.

**2–4 min — Open the Evidence Atlas.** Choose **Casebook**, **Interventions**, **Sources** or **Review gates**. Filter by edition, or type a keyword such as `ceasefire`, `civilian`, `finance` or `law`. Open a card to read its caveats, source and external links. **Pro tip:** a guilty plea, sanctions designation, allegation and manufacturing mark prove different things.

**4–5 min — Compare interventions.** Return to Convergence Map. Select two domains. Check that proposed actions complement rather than merge legal mandates. Read the integration clue and identify the decision owner, lawful prerequisite, civilian impact, metric and stop rule.

**5–7 min — Complete the learner journey.** Pick an eight-class character, follow a cited micro-quest, enter an HTTPS source and an explanatory proof note. Self-reported XP can unlock prerequisite skills in five trees. **Pro tip:** high scores indicate logged learning, *not* professional capability or an externally verified outcome.

**7–9 min — Build a small proposal.** Name one civilian benefit, cite a public source, choose at least two domains, and specify an independently testable effect and stop condition. Save it locally. In the full workshop use five review gates: evidence audit, civilian impact, legal mandate, dissent and outcome verification. All gates remain self-attested and editable.

**9–10 min — Keep or exchange it.** Export a private progress backup. A public handoff strips private character progress and requires deliberate export. A Trystero peer room is online-only, explicit opt-in, and participants' identities are unverified. If it fails, exchange JSON manually; imported work enters a quarantined inbox rather than being automatically trusted.

## Progressive skills

Five skill tracks: **Verification, Care, Coordination, Diplomacy, Systems**. Each has three successive levels. A prerequisite and an available skill point are necessary to unlock the next level. Reopening an earned quest adjusts XP and prevents double-awarding. Each class has preferred activities but does not lock out other classes or useful features.

## HUD and interface clues

- The persistent HUD shows XP, level, earned points, quest progress and motion toggle.
- Small Pro Tip disclosures open in place; no interrupting modal is required.
- Opened Evidence Atlas cards fade into view; controls are keyboard-operable.
- Motion can be disabled and honors reduced-motion preferences.
- The header search indexes records and the learning registry locally.
- The side menu is supplementary, not required to complete the five-phase flow.
- In a disconnected browser, **do not assume progress is saved until the app indicates IndexedDB**. Keep manual exports.
