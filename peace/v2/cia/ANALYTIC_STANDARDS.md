# Public foreign-analysis standards (unofficial)

**Source:** CIA public mission description; ODNI ICD 203. This is an educational adaptation, not a classification or analyst certification system.

1. Define question, geographic/time scope, civilian consequences and factual baseline.
2. Label evidence class: primary finding, judicial record, official designation, party claim, independent reporting, inference, unknown.
3. Examine source access and limitations, independence, publication date, possible interested-party incentives and contrary statements.
4. For each hypothesis provide a mechanism, plausible alternative, disconfirming observation and reversibility/uncertainty statement. Do not invent probabilities.
5. Separate manufacture, commercial export, Russian consignee, actual end use and proved knowledge under applicable law. Model matching ≠ serial matching; designation ≠ criminal conviction.
6. Review lawful mandate, privacy, humanitarian independence, civilian side-effects and whether sharing could facilitate harm.
7. Date corrections, keep unknowns unknown, and only export intentional public-only drafts.

**Do not do:** covert action, clandestine procurement, target identification, offensive cyber, unauthorized surveillance, coercive contact, deceptive influence, policy orders or actions purporting to come from the CIA.
