## Incident adjudication design
A valid agreement must specify incident definitions, authenticated documentation, consent and observer safety. Cases move from report → preservation → lawful independent observation → indeterminate/confirmed/rejected classification → chance for explanation/appeal → protected publication of non-sensitive outcomes. Proposed 48–72h review is only an illustrative term requiring negotiator agreement; no real case processing is integrated. No unilateral consequence or denial of humanitarian care.

## Premortem
Within 24 months, failure may arise through false ceasefire claims, rearmament under a pause, harassment of observers, political capture, leaks of protected routes, coerced aid, biased incident decisions, funding diversion, or recurrences of aggression. Correct with independent review, mandate withdrawal where necessary, safeguarded aid and human-led revision.
