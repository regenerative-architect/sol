# Authority and zero-harm safeguards

**This is not a United Nations service.** UN Charter Article 2(4) prohibits aggression. UN peace operations require actual mandates, consent and resources; the Security Council has primary responsibility and can be subject to political deadlock. [Charter](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text) · [UN peacekeeping principles](https://peacekeeping.un.org/en/principles-of-peacekeeping) · [Mandates](https://peacekeeping.un.org/en/mandates-and-the-legal-basis-for-peacekeeping).

OCHA's humanitarian coordination, OHCHR's human-rights monitoring, UNHCR's protection and UNDP's recovery roles are not interchangeable. The ICRC is independent of the UN; OSCE is separate and its former Special Monitoring Mission ended March 31, 2022. No one may use this prototype to collect personal testimony, military logistics, locations of aid beneficiaries, hospital vulnerabilities, or private financial data.

**Peace versus pause:** temporary cessation is not durable peace; de facto military lines do not affect de jure sovereignty. No action should bypass Ukraine's consent or obstruct its lawful self-defense.

**Information security:** only publicly available source data; no agency endpoints, telemetry, private sync, or live monitoring. Manual signed authenticated exchange would require separate implementation and institutional approval. SHA-256 demonstrates byte identity, not authenticity of a report.
