## Four distinct portfolios
Humanitarian confidence-building; limited/sectoral ceasefires; monitored general ceasefire or armistice; regional security and reconstruction. All entail verification and respect for Ukrainian sovereignty. De facto military positions are not de jure territorial changes.

## 24-month premortem
Data poisoning, interested-party accounts, false ceasefire assurances, sanctions substitution, misattribution of company culpability, compromised humanitarian independence, politicized analytic outputs, privacy leakage, premature economic relief, and unrecognized negotiation changes. Stop rules require independent review and reversible steps.
