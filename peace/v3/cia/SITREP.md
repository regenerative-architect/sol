## Executive finding
The conflict's persistence can be examined through documented incompatibilities over territorial sovereignty, durable security arrangements, expectations of battlefield leverage, ceasefire credibility and multilateral incentives. This does not establish private motives or assign equal legal responsibility for invasion and self-defense.
