# SOL Peace Commons — Fluid Path (v2.1)

## Start here

Open `index.html` and choose **Begin with a finding**. The app shows one dated public reference. You can read it, move on, or optionally switch to a different focus. Then you can write an observation about what the source actually establishes, save a private practical idea, and keep reading. There is no requirement to select a class, earn XP, join a room, or fill out a form.

## The quiet path

**Begin → Explore → Reflect → Build → Continue.** The next step fades into the same workspace, rather than navigating to another website. Each step can be revisited through the thin progress bar. A written reflection or project draft is **optional**. The five research domains, source registry and separate agency-themed editions remain accessible in the full research library and the menu. These are independent, unofficial research tools, not government or UN services.

**Helpful clues** appear as small expandable sections labeled 💡. They are designed for three questions: What does the source establish? What remains uncertain? What could protect civilians? A study note awards local self-reported XP, but neither the XP nor an imported source asserts professional expertise or verified impact.

## For advanced work

Open the **full research library** from the workspace footer, or press the menu button. The original integrated Evidence Atlas, Convergence Map, 8 classes, 15 skills, 16 quests, 5 original institutional editions, cooperative review gates, and explicit opt-in Trystero room feature remain intact. No imported record is automatically verified and no public project is transmitted without consent.

## Offline and data sovereignty

Public research content bundled with the app can be read offline. Browser storage is attempted through IndexedDB, with an honest memory-only fallback when storage is unavailable. Export a JSON backup when you want portable progress. File-based use does not support a service worker. Hosted HTTPS may cache approved public shell resources after a successful visit; uncached external articles and peer-to-peer sessions require connectivity. No account or analytics is required.

## Safety

No violence, targeting, impersonation, coercive outreach, surveillance, offensive cyber, or automatic agency correspondence.
