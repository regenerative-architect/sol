# SOL Peace Commons 2.5 — Auto-minimized Earth Intelligence icon

- On startup, Earth Intelligence appears as a labeled HUD control for roughly 1.7 seconds, then shrinks automatically to a 44×44-pixel globe icon.
- When reduced motion is preferred, it minimizes immediately.
- Click or press Enter/Space on the globe to open the full EIE panel; the icon changes to × when expanded. Click the icon or the panel’s Close button to collapse it.
- The tooltip and accessible label describe the action. The icon remains available through HUD rerenders, route changes and screen resizing.
- Icon-only mode is presentation-only; no extra network requests, broadcasts, permission changes or data mutations. Trystero retains the existing independent connection switch.
- Refreshed the service-worker shell namespace to `sol-peace-master-shell-v8`. The IndexedDB research and backup schema is unchanged.
